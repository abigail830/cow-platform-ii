## PAYROLL SERVICES

### SCOPE OF SERVICES

#### A. Payroll Computation and Payroll Register Generation

1. Configuring pay code taxability per employee;
2. Maintenance of tax table (Withholding tax table uploaded by BIR); and
3. Determination of contributions to SSS, Philhealth, and Home Development and Mutual Fund (HDMF).
4. Pay slips Distribution
5. Salary disbursement

Payroll Register shall contain individual salaries due to each employee with the corresponding payroll taxes withheld and deductions for SSS, Philhealth, and HDMF for each period.

Note: The Client's HR unit or equivalent shall provide regular timesheet details of the employees, stating the number of man-hours worked, overtime hours, tardiness, and absences.

The rates shall apply to a maximum of two (2) payroll runs per month. In case the Client requires the Consultant to perform additional payroll runs for a particular month for any reason other than errors committed by the Consultant and will generate separate payroll registers other than the two regular payroll runs, such will be considered as Special Payroll Runs.

#### B. Special Payroll Runs

Consultant can provide Special Payroll Run Services if requested by the Client, such as Final Pay Computation, Separate Bonus/ 13th Month Pay Runs, etc., or any other run that will generate separate Payroll Register and/or Payroll-related Reports other than those mentioned above. The consultant will bill the Client a separate fee equivalent to the number of employees to be included in the generated payroll register/report.

The consultant will bill the Client a separate fee equivalent to our fee schedule based on the number of employees.

#### C. Preparation of remittances to government agencies, as follows:

1. **Social Security System:** Monthly remittance using R5/PRN form
2. **Philhealth Insurance:** Monthly report via EPRS
3. **Home Development Mutual Fund (PAG-IBIG Fund):** Monthly remittance report/ E-Gov
4. **Bureau of Internal Revenue:**
   - i. Tax Return for Compensation (BIR Form # 1601C and BIR Form #1604C)
   - ii. Alphabetical listing (Alphalist Schedule 1 and 2) at year-end for resigned and non-resigned employees. This includes total salaries received per employee, total tax due, withheld and remitted to the Bureau of Internal Revenue.
   - iii. Preparation of BIR Form # 2316 for individual employees as proof of income tax payment.

#### D. Preparation of 13th-month pay and other bonuses as part of regularly generated Payroll Register
