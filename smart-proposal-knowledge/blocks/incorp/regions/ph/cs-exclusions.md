### EXCLUSIONS OF SERVICE

- Appointment as Corporate Treasurer;
- Signing of monthly, quarterly, and annual tax reports for the Bureau of Internal Revenue;
- Signing of Statement of Management Responsibility for Financial Statements and Income Tax;
- Signing of monthly contributions and other related reports to Social Security System, Philippine Health Insurance Corporation, and Home Development Mutual Fund; and
- Work requiring extensive study and/or documentation or legal opinions.
- Attendance in hearings with judicial and quasi-judicial bodies of the government for internal matters of the Corporation.
- Signing of any document or paper that relates to the operations of the company
