## CORPORATE SECRETARIAL AND RESIDENT Agent SERVICES

Maintaining a Corporate Secretary (CS) is mandatory under Philippine Corporate Law for the execution and certification of documents and proceedings of the Board of Directors. The absence of a CS can result in the corporation being placed under SEC Monitoring and fines.

### SCOPE OF SERVICE

- Preparation of minutes of meetings of the stockholders and board of directors;
- Issuance of corporate secretary's certificates, board resolutions, other certifications;
- Documentation and reporting of constitutional changes such as changes in the company name, principal address, financial year, increase in capital stock, etc.;
- Documentation of simple share transfers, reversions of trust, and issuance of shares;
- Preparation and filing of annual General Information of Sheet or any amendment thereof with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission;
- Preparation of documents to release the audited financial statements (if applicable);
- Maintenance of stock and transfer book;
- Printing of stock certificates subject to a separate fee;
- Regular communication and updates; and
- Communication on compliance requirements under Philippine Law.
