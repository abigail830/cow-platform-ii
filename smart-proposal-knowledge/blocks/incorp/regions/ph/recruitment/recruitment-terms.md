## GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS

### A. INFORMATION AND CONFIDENTIALITY

1. The Client is bound to furnish ITS with any potentially important information that might reasonably relate to the employment of candidates referred. All information shall be treated as confidential.
2. The Client is obliged to comply with the Philippine Labor and Employment laws with respect to the treatment of the candidate referred by ITS and shall treat the information provided on account of said candidate as confidential.
3. ITS and the Client are obliged to keep confidential all information, which has been indicated as such in writing, that was obtained from each other in the framework of their agreement, or from another source.

### B. INDEPENDENCE

1. The client shall independently ascertain the suitability of the candidate before entering into an employment contract. ITS will take all reasonable precautions prior to and during the selection, but this does not absolve the Client of its investigational obligation.

### C. TERM AND VALIDITY

1. This Agreement shall be valid for one (1) year from effective date and will auto-renew to the next year unless a written notice from either party is received by the other at least thirty (30) days before intended date of termination or there are changes in the respective signatories..
2. In case the Client hires a candidate referred by ITS within twelve (12) months from the date of endorsement, this shall be considered as ITS's placement and will be subject to professional fee.
3. ITS reserves the right to refuse to furnish a replacement candidate if the payment terms under this Agreement are not met.

### D. NON-SOLICITATION

1. ITS shall refrain from approaching the client's employee(s) with the intention of soliciting such employee(s) for another employer, unless such employee takes the initiative by demonstrably making it known that he/she wishes to accept a position elsewhere.
2. The Client shall, for the duration of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year from its termination, refrain from approaching ITS's employee(s) with the intention of soliciting such employee(s). The Parties agree that the solicitation of ITS employee(s) shall be cause for liquidated damages in the amount of three (3) times the cost of the recruitment for said employee(s).

### E. RESTRAINT OF TRADE

1. If the Client declines to accept the candidate(s) presented by ITS or the candidate(s) refuses the Client's offer within twelve (12) months from date of endorsement, and an employment contract is nevertheless concluded between candidate and Client, thus shall be considered as ITS's placement and the Client shall be liable to pay the professional fee, which will not affect the right of ITS to claim additional compensation where additional damage can be proven.
2. The Client is prohibited from passing or introducing candidates on to third parties. If the Client violates this prohibition, he shall be obliged to pay liquidated damages in the amount of double the professional fee. This is applicable from the time of the initial contact between the Client and the third party, even before the actual assignment commences.

### F. FEES

1. In case the Client hires the candidate as an independent contractor. Consultant or on project-based employment, other than a direct hire, the Client agrees to pay 25% of the estimated earnings for the entire project, which is exclusive of other benefits, monetary or otherwise.
2. In case the Client hires the candidate on a full-time employment on or after the lapse of the consultancy or project term, the Client agrees to pay 18%/20% for respective roles and levels stated in Section of the remaining months of the 12-month period that will serve as basis for imposing the fee of ITS.
3. In case the Client decides to cancel the hiring of ITS's candidate for reasons other than negative reference checks, health conditions, criminal records, and/or falsification of supporting documents (transcript of records, COE, etc.), after the candidate has formally signed a job offer, the Client agrees to pay ITS a professional fee equivalent to two (2) months' salary based on the selected candidate's offer to cover operational costs, as well as one (1) month salary to be paid to the candidate.

### G. DUPLICATION

1. In order to avoid duplication of candidates between ITS and the Client, the Client agrees to inform ITS after one (1) business day from receipt of endorsements if the candidate(s) are already in the Client's database and is currently undergoing the Client's recruitment process. The Client and ITS agree to recognize that the candidate(s) are tagged as ITS's should they fall under the following conditions:
   - a. Candidate submitted application to Client but has not received acknowledgment and/or invitation for interview;
   - b. Candidate was invited by Client but has declined or has withdrawn his/her application altogether;
   - c. Candidate was processed but has decided to withdraw his/her application;
   - d. Candidate submitted application and was processed by Client but has never received any communication thereafter; and/or
   - e. Candidate's application was processed but has not received any communication for the last six (6) months.

### H. DATA PRIVACY

1. By signing this proposal, the Client, as a Data Subject, expressly gives their consent to ITS to collect, process, endorse, share, store, retain, update, and retrieve information, personal or otherwise, provided solely for the purpose of performing the services described in this proposal. The Client also consents to the sharing of their data with ITS' affiliates unless expressly stated otherwise. The Client confirms that they were made aware that the data collected are necessary for the effective performance of the services described in this proposal and that said data will be securely stored by ITS for as long it deems necessary, and it will be destroyed upon express request and consent of the Client. The processing of the collected information may be made through an internal database, online, cloud storage, or through a sub-contractor. Finally, the Client confirms that they were made aware of their rights under the Data Privacy Act and other pertinent laws.

### I. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is to be interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

### J. VENUE AND JURISDICTION

Any dispute or controversy arising under or in connection with this agreement shall be settled solely and exclusively in the proper court of Taguig City, to the exclusion of all other courts.

### K. LIABILITY

ITS is in no way liable for damages and losses caused by candidates who were hired through its services.
