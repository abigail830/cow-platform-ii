### B. GUARANTEE PERIOD

If the candidate resigns or is terminated by the Client for reasons other than retrenchment, redundancy, unlawful or constructive dismissal before completing three (3) months of service for full-time roles, ITS shall provide a single replacement candidate, whether for the same role or a different role for as long as this Agreement is valid. Difference of cost from the original invoice to the replaced candidate shall be billed accordingly. In cases of termination, the Client is expected to have exercised due process according to the provisions of the Philippine Labor Law. ITS's commitment to this context is the replacement of the successful candidate and not a refund of any portion of the professional search fee. If, for some reason, the client decides to waive the option of requiring a replacement, ITS shall be relieved of said commitment.
