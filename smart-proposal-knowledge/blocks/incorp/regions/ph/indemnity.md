## INDEMNITY

Under Philippine Corporate Law, corporate officers (President, Treasurer, Secretary, etc.) may be held administratively, civilly, and/or criminally responsible for any unlawful act or omission committed by the corporation through its various agents, regardless if the position/s is/are occupied merely as an accommodation or with a fee. Accordingly, the Client undertakes to indemnify InCorp PH and/or the specific person appointed by the Client to hold the position from all liabilities, costs or expenses arising from any and against all actions, proceedings, claims, or demands of any nature which may be made against InCorp PH or on account of being elected as an officer of the Client, or by reason of any act, deed or any matter done or omitted to be done in his/her capacity as officer under the Client's instructions. The Client (or its lawful representatives) further commits to inform in writing InCorp PH and the person/s concerned of any event or change of circumstance that may affect the appointment, such as, without limitation, change of principal, retirement of business, et. al.
