### TERMS

- This service should be available for a minimum one (1) year and shall commence on the first day of the month provided in the Certificate of Incorporation or on the first day of the month from the signing of the proposal. IN.CORP PH will bill CS services quarterly in advance and the client shall pay for the same quarterly. In case of pre-termination initiated by the Client, there shall be a pre-termination fee equivalent to twenty-five percent (25%) of the service fee for the unexpired term of the agreement.
- The service shall only begin upon receipt of full payment of initial CS service fee, related initial deposit for expenses for the first year, and after InCorp PH's due diligence has been completed.
- The highest-ranking officer of the company shall sign an undated acceptance to the resignation letter of the Corporate Secretary;
- Client must post a refundable Cash Deposit of PHP200,000.00 upon engagement for Corporate Secretarial Services. This deposit may be refunded once the Client submits a Director/Officer Liability Insurance Policy. The cash deposit shall be returned six (6) months after the termination of the service or upon submission of the duly filed General Information Sheet (GIS) reflecting the new corporate secretary, whichever comes first;
- All documents requested by the Client shall be provided to the Client in PDF form;
- Either party may terminate this agreement at any time for any reason, upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. However, invoices will continue to be sent to Client until we receive the SEC filed General Information Sheet reflecting the new Corporate Secretary.
- This proposal shall be valid within six (6) months from the issuance thereof.
