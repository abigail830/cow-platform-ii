## SERVICES AND FEES

This section provides a comprehensive overview of InCorp's Professional Fees, along with other incidental expenses and the estimated government fees, which are calculated either based on specific tasks or on a per-case basis. Please note that any work will commence only upon receipt of your written approval to proceed.

All fees mentioned are in Philippine Pesos, exclusive charges of remitting, intermediary, and receiving banks.

Out-of-pocket and Transportation costs are at a fixed charge for the entire visa process within NCR and Cebu.
