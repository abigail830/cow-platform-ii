## Nominee Director Service

- The PHP 200,000 securities deposit applies for both Corporate Secretarial and Nominee Director services. This is a lump sum deposit per client.
- Refundable six (6) months from the cessation of Nominee Director Service or submission of the Director Liability Insurance Policy.
- Appointment of one nominee Director for one (1) share;
- Preparation of Declaration of Trust and Deed of Assignment (DOTDA);
- Preparation of Termination of Trust;
- Preparation of Resignation Letters; and
- Execution of documents such as Minutes and Resolutions for the Board of Directors as Nominees (subject to review).
