## Corporate Treasurer Service

### 1. Corporate Treasurer – for SEC incorporation

- Acting as Acting Treasurer for the sole purpose of SEC registration, including the opening of the mandatory Treasurer-in-Trust Fund (TITF) bank account. The Treasurer must be a resident of the Philippines. The paid-up capital must be infused into the TITF account prior to the release of the Certificate of Incorporation by the SEC;
- Preparation of Acceptance Letter for appointment as Treasurer.
- Preparation of Resignation Letter as Treasurer.

Note: The SEC requires that the capital be infused before the end of the company's fiscal year, but if we are engaged as Corporate Treasurer our management requires us to open a TITF account where the capital must be infused prior to SEC incorporation to ensure compliance with this requirement and avoid penalties.

### 2. Corporate Treasurer – Post SEC incorporation

Signing of documents required to be signed by the Corporate Treasurer including the following:

- Signing of monthly, quarterly, and annual tax reports for the Bureau of Internal Revenue;
- Signing of Statement of Management Responsibility for Financial Statements and Income Tax; and
- Signing of monthly contributions and its related report to Social Security System, Philippine Health Insurance Corporation, and Home Development Mutual Fund
