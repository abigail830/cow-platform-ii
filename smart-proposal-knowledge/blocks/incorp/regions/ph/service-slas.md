### DEPOSIT FOR EXPENSES

InCorp PH shall provide an expense report for Government Fees (GF) actually charged. Government expenses must be adequately funded to begin work. The Client or InCorp PH shall settle any deficiency or excess, respectively.

### REFUND POLICY

Upon confirmation of the Client of the engagement and the payment of all fees, USD 200 shall be deducted from the total amount of payment made by the Client for professional fees. The remainder shall be refunded based on the table below:

| Work covered | Refund |
|--------------|--------|
| Name Reservation | 90% of the remaining Professional Fee |
| After Drafting of Corporate Documents | 40% of the remaining Professional Fee |
| After Issuance of License to Transact Business in the Philippines | 20% of the remaining Professional Fee |
| After commencement of work with LGU and/or the remaining government agencies | 10% of the remaining Professional Fee |

Notice of cancellation must be made via e-mail to the point of contact. A cancellation acknowledgment will be sent within three (3) working days, and fees will be refunded within sixty (60) working days.
