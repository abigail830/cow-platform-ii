## GENERAL BOOKKEEPING AND TAX COMPLIANCE

### A. Minimum Term

The Services provided herein shall be on a continuing engagement between the Client and In.Corp. However, the Client agrees that this engagement shall be effective for at least twelve (12) months from the time of commencement.

### B. Fee Arrangement

In.Corp shall require payment within 7 days after receipt of invoice or billing statement. Fees should be received net of bank charges, all applicable taxes and charges. Deficiency as a result of bank charges shall be for the account of the client.

The Client shall pay a three percent (3%) surcharge by way of penalty every month when fees are not paid on time, computed from the time the amount is due until full payment.

### C. Re-Assessment of Fees

In.Corp reserves the right to re-assess the fees at the end of each quarter of the calendar year. However, In.Corp shall not implement any increase in the fees unless agreed upon in writing by the Client.

### D. Client's Responsibilities

1. In.Corp's work will be based solely on the information provided, the circumstances made known to In.Corp and the assumptions set out in our correspondence. In.Corp relies on the Client to bring to In.Corp's attention as soon as possible any changes in the information as originally presented.
2. The timely performance of the Services and meeting the deadlines set by the Client or government agencies depends on timely receipt of accurate data and/or funds from the Client at the times requested.
3. During the engagement, In.Corp will adhere to the points of contact provided herein.
4. The Client shall prepare checks for payments to the BIR and other government agencies.

However, In.Corp may arrange for the Client to avail of "use of bank" services for a separate fee.

### E. Ownership of Work Product

The Client understands that all work products prepared by In.Corp shall become the property of the Client only upon full payment by the Client of amounts billed by In.Corp for services rendered ("Outstanding Bills"). In.Corp shall also have the right to retain in its possession all work products prepared and documents provided by Client under this Service Agreement in the performance of the Services until full payment of all Outstanding Bills.

### F. Termination of Services

This Agreement may be terminated by either party, by issuing a written notice of termination, through regular correspondence address or email address of the other party marked for the appropriate partner or contact, 30 days before the effectivity date.

In the event that the Client decides to terminate the engagement after the signing of this proposal, InCorp PH shall have the right to bill the work done and time spent from the commencement of engagement up to termination of service. Actual expenses incurred will be charged to the client.
