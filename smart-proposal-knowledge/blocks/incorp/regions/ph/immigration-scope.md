## IMMIGRATION

### SCOPE OF SERVICE

- Preparation and drafting of documentary requirements needed for the above applications (except for documents that need the authentication/apostille of the Philippine Embassy/Consulate);
- Monitoring and updating of valid stay of the applicant during the application;
- All related leg work like delivery of documents and passport to and from the client or the subject/s;
- Consultation and reporting on the status of the application; and
- Meeting and follow-up with pertinent government officials/officers.

### EXCLUSIONS OF SERVICE

- Drafting of Invitation Letter for Entry Visa application
- Procurement of Entry Visa for Visa Required Nationals;
- Apostille or authentication of documents obtained abroad;
- Annual Report, Cancellation of previous AEP and I-Card, Amendment or Correction of Arrival Stamp, Visa Restamping, and other services not expressly stated in this Agreement.
