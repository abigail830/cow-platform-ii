### Company formation

We provide comprehensive company formation services that put you on pace for a fast and successful incorporation. Our services include determination of company name availability, complete oversight of documentation and the dispatch process. Our skilled team has the capacity to quickly execute demands to meet even the tightest of deadlines.

We are leaders in company formation services throughout the world's most business-friendly jurisdictions, offering cost-effective solutions through a variety of corporate vehicles. Our secretarial and administrative support is available to extend beyond the incorporation stage to assist you throughout the life of the business.

### Registered agent / registered office

We provide both registered agent/ registered office services in the BVI. Our registered office and registered agent services not only provide companies and limited partnerships with a physical address, but also ensure proper maintenance of the entity's records, registers, and other required documents.

Our professional team members at all our global offices understand the nuanced differences required of each jurisdiction and entity type.

### Compliance solutions

We offer a range of solutions for offshore compliance where it comes to anti-money laundering and terrorist financing compliance, financial services regulation, sanctions, tax, tax information exchange, and other areas relating to financial services.

### AEOI compliance services

Our experts across the world assist clients in creating solutions that ensure compliance with legal regimes such as US FATCA and UK FATCA. We partner with clients to navigate applicable FATCA regulations and correctly classify both accounts and investors. Our team works with companies and funds in the BVI to ensure proper compliance with Automatic Exchange of Information (AEOI). We appoint individuals to the appropriate AEOI roles relevant to your jurisdiction and register as needed with the IRS and for online reporting of the ITA or TIA.

We're experienced in implementing AEOI policies and procedures, including required disclosures where needed and reporting on an annual basis. Our team is adept at removing the burden of compliance for our clients. We also minimise risk by ensuring all the requirements are met in full and on time.
