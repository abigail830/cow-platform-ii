### Services and documents not included in the annual maintenance costs

Costs not covered by our annual maintenance fee:

Capital contribution: Fees to prepare resolutions for capital contributions will be estimated by our strategic alliance partner Harneys law firm. The costs will depend on the complexity of each case, however, for simple resolutions involving contributions only in cash, the fee will be $650 + disbursements of $150 to update the Register of Members.

Redemption or distributions of dividends: Fees to prepare resolutions for redemption or distributions of dividends will be estimated by strategic alliance partner Harneys law firm. The costs will depend on the complexity of each case, however, for simple resolutions involving redemptions/distributions only in cash, the fee will be $850 + disbursements of $150 to update the Register of Members.

Certificate of Good Standing: Certificate confirming that the company is in good standing and has met its statutory obligations. The fee is $170. The BVI Registry charges an additional $50 to provide a physical (hard copy) Certificate of Good Standing (if requested).

Certificate of Incumbency: Certificate confirming the current shareholders and directors of the company. The fee is $300.

W-8 BEN-E (FATCA/CRS): To complete the W-8 BEN-E form, the company will need to be classified for FATCA/CRS purposes. To determine the applicable fees, we will require details of the company's asset types and management structure.

Notarisation with Apostille: Notarisation with Apostille of documents. The fee is $650 + courier costs. This fee does not include the preparation of Power of Attorney (PoA).

Courier: We use DHL for courier services. The fee is $100 per document shipment. Courier charges are not mandatory and will only apply if physical documents are requested (except for incorporations).
