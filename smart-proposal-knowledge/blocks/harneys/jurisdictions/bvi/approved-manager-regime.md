The BVI Approved Manager regime allows eligible investment managers and advisers to be regulated under a simple approval process using a cost-effective and flexible framework, backed by the credibility of the BVI Financial Services Commission (FSC).

Key benefits include:

- Fast approval – applications are typically approved within seven days
- Lower costs – reduced setup and ongoing compliance expenses
- Light regulation – streamlined requirements allow managers to focus on core activities
- High AUM limits – up to US$400m (open-ended) and US$1bn (closed-ended)
- Global flexibility – ability to manage BVI and non-BVI funds
- Flexible structures – accommodates various legal and operational models
- Reduced overheads – minimal local infrastructure required
- Strong reputation – a stable, well-regulated, and respected international jurisdiction

The BVI Approved Manager regime delivers the right balance of credibility, flexibility, and efficiency for managers looking to establish and grow their business.
