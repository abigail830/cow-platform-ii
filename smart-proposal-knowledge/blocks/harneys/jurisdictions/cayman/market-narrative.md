The Cayman Islands is the fifth-largest banking centre in the world and one of the world's leading international financial centres. This highly regulated and politically stable jurisdiction offers a wide array of services including mutual funds, company management, structured financing, shipping registration, insurance, and securities listings on the stock exchange.

Since the introduction of the Mutual Funds Law in 1993 (now the Mutual Funds Act), which has been replicated by jurisdictions around the world, the Cayman Islands has grown to be the world's leading offshore investment funds jurisdiction.

Cayman benefits from an internationally experienced and qualified workforce as well as experienced lawyers, accountants, and auditors. As a British Overseas Territory, Cayman's laws are based largely on the laws of the United Kingdom, providing a familiar and credible legal framework for institutional and sophisticated investors.

For regulated investment funds such as the Fund and its SP, this environment offers a robust, well-regarded and business-friendly framework, with mature regulation, access to first-class professional advisers, and an efficient regulator in CIMA.
