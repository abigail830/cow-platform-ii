[/body/p[@paraId=3D811836]] CORPORATE SECRETARIAL AND RESIDENT Agent SERVICES 
[/body/p[@paraId=1C8A948E]] 
[/body/p[@paraId=0B71C343]] Maintaining a Corporate Secretary (CS) is mandatory under Philippine Corporate Law for the execution and certification of documents and proceedings of the Board of Directors. The absence of a CS can result in the corporation being placed under SEC Monitoring and fines.

[/body/p[@paraId=41383994]] ● Issuance of corporate secretary’s certificates, board resolutions, other certifications;
[/body/p[@paraId=2505B976]] ● Documentation and reporting of constitutional changes such as changes in the company name, principal address, financial year, increase in capital stock, etc.;
[/body/p[@paraId=67A89D69]] ● Documentation of simple share transfers, reversions of trust, and issuance of shares;
[/body/p[@paraId=4AABB22A]] ● Preparation and filing of annual General Information of Sheet or any amendment thereof with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission;
[/body/p[@paraId=37DEDA1D]] ● Preparation of documents to release the audited financial statements (if applicable);
[/body/p[@paraId=7B6250BD]] ● Maintenance of stock and transfer book; 
[/body/p[@paraId=05C06A6F]] ● Printing of stock certificates subject to a separate fee; 
[/body/p[@paraId=41BFDCE5]] ● Regular communication and updates; and
[/body/p[@paraId=788A1C59]] ● Communication on compliance requirements under Philippine Law.

[/body/p[@paraId=11D87D1E]] INDEMNITY
[/body/p[@paraId=3944B05B]] 
[/body/p[@paraId=1B2E5F57]] Under Philippine Corporate Law, corporate officers (President, Treasurer, Secretary, etc.) may be held administratively, civilly, and/or criminally responsible for any unlawful act or omission committed by the corporation through its various agents, regardless if the position/s is/are occupied merely as an accommodation or with a fee. Accordingly, the Client undertakes to indemnify InCorp PH and/or the specific person appointed by the Client to hold the position from all liabilities, costs or expenses arising from any and against all actions, proceedings, claims, or demands of any nature which may be made against InCorp PH or on account of being elected as an officer of the Client, or by reason of any act, deed or any matter done or omitted to be done in his/her capacity as officer under the Client’s instructions. The Client (or its lawful representatives) further commits to inform in writing InCorp PH and the person/s concerned of any event or change of circumstance that may affect the appointment, such as, without limitation, change of principal, retirement of business, et. al.
