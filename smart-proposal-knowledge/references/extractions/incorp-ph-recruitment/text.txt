[/body/p[@paraId=29E5337F]] 1.	This Agreement shall be valid for one (1) year from effective date and will auto-renew to the next year unless a written notice from either party is received by the other at least thirty (30) days before intended date of termination or there are changes in the respective signatories..

[/body/p[@paraId=2D430A05]] 3.	ITS reserves the right to refuse to furnish a replacement candidate if the payment terms under this Agreement are not met.

[/body/p[@paraId=714773E8]] D. NON-SOLICITATION
[/body/p[@paraId=5D369107]] 1.	ITS shall refrain from approaching the client's employee(s) with the intention of soliciting such employee(s) for another employer, unless such employee takes the initiative by demonstrably making it known that he/she wishes to accept a position elsewhere.
[/body/p[@paraId=11669C3A]] 2.	The Client shall, for the duration of this Agreement and for a period of one (1) year from its termination, refrain from approaching ITS’s employee(s) with the intention of soliciting such employee(s). The Parties agree that the solicitation of ITS employee(s) shall be cause for liquidated damages in the amount of three (3) times the cost of the recruitment for said employee(s).
[/body/p[@paraId=28841904]] 
[/body/p[@paraId=6EC06DEA]] E. RESTRAINT OF TRADE
[/body/p[@paraId=6745A470]] 1. If the Client declines to accept the candidate(s) presented by ITS or the candidate(s) refuses the Client’s offer within twelve (12) months from date of endorsement, and an employment contract is nevertheless concluded between candidate and Client, thus shall be considered as ITS’s placement and the Client shall be liable to pay the professional fee, which will not affect the right of ITS to claim additional compensation where additional damage can be proven.
[/body/p[@paraId=75C2553F]] 2. The Client is prohibited from passing or introducing candidates on to third parties. If the Client violates this prohibition, he shall be obliged to pay liquidated damages in the amount of double the professional fee. This is applicable from the time of the initial contact between the Client and the third party, even before the actual assignment commences.

In case the Client hires the candidate on a full-time employment on or after the lapse of the consultancy or project term, the Client agrees to pay 18%/20% for respective roles and levels stated in Section of the remaining months of the 12-month period that will serve as basis for imposing the fee of ITS.
[/body/p[@paraId=7F79AAA0]] 3.	In case the Client decides to cancel the hiring of ITS’s candidate for reasons other than negative reference checks, health conditions, criminal records, and/or falsification of supporting documents (transcript of records, COE, etc.), after the candidate has formally signed a job offer, the Client agrees to pay ITS a professional fee equivalent to two (2) months’ salary based on the selected candidate’s offer to cover operational costs, as well as one (1) month salary to be paid to the candidate.

[/body/p[@paraId=3F094ED4]] G. DUPLICATION
[/body/p[@paraId=6321E664]] 1. In order to avoid duplication of candidates between ITS and the Client, the Client agrees to inform ITS after one (1) business day from receipt of endorsements if the candidate(s) are already in the Client’s database and is currently undergoing the Client’s recruitment process. The Client and ITS agree to recognize that the candidate(s) are tagged as ITS’s should they fall under the following conditions:
[/body/p[@paraId=26908031]]   a. Candidate submitted application to Client but has not received acknowledgment and/or invitation for interview;

[/body/p[@paraId=065E06D0]]   e. Candidate’s application was processed but has not received any communication for the last six (6) months.
