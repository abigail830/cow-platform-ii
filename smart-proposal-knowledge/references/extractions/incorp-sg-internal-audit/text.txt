Our Audit Methodology

The International Professional Practices Framework® (IPPF®) organises the authoritative body of knowledge promulgated by the IIA for the professional practice of internal auditing.
















The Institute of Internal Auditors’ Global Internal Audit Standards guide the worldwide professional practice of internal auditing and serve as a basis for evaluating and elevating the quality of the internal audit function. 
At the heart of the Standards are 15 guiding principles that enable effective internal auditing. 
Each principle is supported by standards that contain requirements, considerations for implementation, and examples of evidence of conformance. 
Domain I
Purpose of Internal Auditing
Domain II
Ethics and Professionalism
Domain III
Governing the Internal Audit Function
Domain IV
Managing the Internal Audit Function
Domain V
Performing Internal Audit Services

Internal Audit Methodology and Approach
OUR APPROACH TO INTERNAL AUDIT
Review and evaluate the adequacy and effectiveness of internal controls in the identified process.
Identify non-conformance, gaps, risks and potential implications in the process.
Make recommendations to improve internal controls for the identified areas of inadequacies or weakness.
Identify areas of improvement in terms of efficiency, economy and effectiveness and provide reasonable and practical recommendations.
